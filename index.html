The New York Giants are a professional American football team based in New York. The Giants compete in the NFL as a member of the NFC East division. The team plays its home games at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, five miles west of New York City. The Giants are headquartered and practice at the Quest Diagnostics Training Center, also in the Meadowlands.
